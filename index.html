HELP & SUPPORT

Answers when you need them.

Find the next step for setup, server selection, and connection issues.

How do I install SaSa?

Download SaSa VPN from Google Play and follow Android’s VPN setup prompt when you connect.

How do I connect to a server?

Open Servers, select an available location, return to Home, and tap Connect. Wait for the app to show Connected.

What if the connection fails?

Check your internet connection and the selected server, then try again. If a custom profile is in use, confirm its endpoint and credentials.

Can I import my own VPN profile?

Open Settings, find My VPN Profiles, and import a supported file, link, or configuration. Only use profiles from providers you trust.

Can I use SaSa on multiple devices?

Device limits depend on your plan. See your active plan in the app for the current limit.

Where can I view plans?

See your plan in the app. If your account is linked to Telegram, you can also view supported plans in the SaSa bot.

Where are connection settings?

Open Settings, then Connection Settings. Available options can vary by device and Android version.
