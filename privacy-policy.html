Privacy Policy

For the Sasa VPN Android and iOS apps. Last updated October 28, 2026.

This notice explains information handled by Sasa VPN and the Google Mobile Ads services used in the app.

Account and entitlement data

Sasa VPN stores the account, subscription, device, profile, expiry, and access information needed to authenticate the service and enforce plan limits. If you choose a third-party sign-in option where available, the provider handles authentication under its own privacy policy; Sasa receives the account information needed to create and secure your account.

VPN usage data

For managed mobile sessions, the app reports cumulative upload and download bytes. The backend stores accepted usage increments with the time they were checked. Sasa does not intentionally collect browsing history or DNS query history. The VPN server operator can process connection metadata and traffic that passes through that server.

Operational records

Connection events, audit events, provisioning state, and support requests help protect and operate the VPN service. The app may make brief connection checks to show server availability and latency.

Advertising and privacy choices

Sasa uses Google Mobile Ads for rewarded videos that grant 15 minutes of free VPN access and banner ads during eligible unpaid VPN connections, including guest and imported-profile sessions. A new Guest may make one initial connection for up to 60 minutes without a rewarded ad; later sessions can require a completed rewarded video before connecting. The session timer starts after the VPN engine confirms connection, and disconnecting consumes any unused time. Current access limits are shown in the app.

Google Mobile Ads may collect the device IP address, ad and app interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and advertising or device identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The app does not send VPN tunnel contents, browsing history, or DNS queries in ad requests. Google and its selected ad partners may use data for personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads based on your region and choices. When required, you can review or change ad privacy choices in Settings.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents and records consent choices. You can reopen available choices in Settings → Privacy choices. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's data practices.

If DNS resolution prevents an ad from loading, the app may use a temporary DNS-only fallback for Google ad hostnames. Those selected DNS lookups may be sent to Google Public DNS over HTTPS, which can receive the queried hostname and network address. This fallback does not carry browsing traffic or other DNS queries.

Your choices

You can choose whether to watch a rewarded ad and can manage available advertising privacy choices in the app. Contact Sasa VPN support for access, correction, or deletion requests. Do not send passwords, verification codes, VPN configuration files, or private keys in a support message.

Retention and account deletion

Information is kept as needed to provide and secure the service, handle support requests, and meet legal obligations. Sasa VPN does not publish a fixed permanent-deletion deadline for every record. After a verified account-deletion request is processed, access tokens are revoked and the account is soft-deleted; some operational, security, usage, support, or legally required records may remain.

To ask a privacy question or request account deletion, contact Sasa VPN support on Telegram. See the account and data deletion instructions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice as the service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
